# Cardgame

## Combat

- Play units from your hand onto your bench.
- On the round you hold the Attack Token, select a ready unit and choose **Attack**.
- Drag each attacker from your bench into the combat zone, then choose **Confirm Attack**. Drag an attacker back to its bench to remove it from the group, or choose **Cancel**.
- Before ending your turn, drag an unused unit you played this turn down into the hand area to recall it and refund its gold cost. This also works for an attacker still waiting in the combat zone; units that have acted cannot be recalled.
- During an enemy attack, drag a ready unit directly onto the attacker you want to block. You can also click your blocker, then click the attacker. Each blocker can stop only one attacker.
- Drag an assigned blocker back to its bench to remove that block.
- Choose **Resolve Combat** when your blocks are ready. Blocked units exchange damage simultaneously; attackers without blockers strike your Nexus.
- When you confirm an attack, a lethal Human Warrior or Human Ranger hit against a blocking unit plays its unique execution cinematic before combat damage resolves. The AI prioritizes a killable blocker for these attacks so the executions can be seen; unblocked Nexus attacks do not trigger them.
